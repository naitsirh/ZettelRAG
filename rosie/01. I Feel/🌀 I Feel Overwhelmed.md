# 🌀 **I Feel Overwhelmed**

Hi Roro. 💖  
It’s all too much right now, isn’t it?

Too loud.  
Too fast.  
Too many people.  
Too many feelings.  

You don’t have to fix it all at once.  
You don’t even have to fix it today.  

We’re going to slow the world down together.  
One breath. One thought. One tiny step.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:

- “I feel like I’m being buried under everything at once.”  
- “It’s too loud, too bright, too much.”  
- “I don’t know what to do first, so I freeze.”  
- “My chest feels tight. I can’t think clearly.”  
- “I want to run away from everything.”  
- “I feel like I’m letting everyone down.”  
- “Even small things feel impossible.”

---

## 💌 You’re Not Alone

Feeling overwhelmed doesn’t mean you’re weak.  
It means you’ve been carrying too much for too long.  

Your brain is saying, *“I need help to hold all of this.”*  
We can share the weight together.

---

## ✋ Step 1: Pause the World

If it’s safe—say it out loud or whisper it to yourself:

> “Stop. I need a moment.”
> "[[🍯 Syrup]]"

You are **allowed** to pause.  
You are **allowed** to step away.  
You are **allowed** to *not be okay* right now.

---

## 🌬️ Step 2: Let Your Breath Hold You

- Sit or stand still.  
- Put your hand on your chest or stomach.  
- Breathe in slowly through your nose (count 1…2…3…).  
- Hold it gently for 2 seconds.  
- Breathe out slowly through your mouth (count 1…2…3…4…5…).  

Do this once.  
Then, if you want, do it again.  
That’s already enough.

---

## 🌼 Step 3: Affirmations

Say these slowly, even if you don’t believe them yet:  

> “I can take this one small step at a time.”  
> “I do not have to do everything right now.”  
> “It’s okay to rest before I continue.”  
> “The world can wait for me to catch up.”
