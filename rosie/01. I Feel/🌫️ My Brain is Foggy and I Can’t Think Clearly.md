# 🌫️ My Brain is Foggy and I Can’t Think Clearly

Hi love. 💖  
If you’re here, your brain is **doing its best**, even if it doesn’t feel that way.

Sometimes fog rolls in:  
Thoughts drift away.  
Words won’t land.  
You may feel **slow**, **dumb**, or **frustrated**.

Let’s be clear:

💬 **You are not stupid.**  
💤 **You are not lazy.**  
🧠 **Your brain is tired, not broken.**

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:

- “I can’t focus.”  
- “I know what I want to say, but I can’t say it.”  
- “I keep losing my train of thought.”  
- “My head feels full of cotton.”  
- “I keep rereading the same thing.”  
- “I feel dumb even though I know I’m not.”

---

## 💌 You’re Not Alone

This happens when the brain is tired, stressed, overloaded, or trying to protect you.  
It’s a **state**, not a failure.

You don’t have to “push through” — we’ll move gently together.

---

## 🧭 Step 1: Anchor to the Now

- Touch something soft.  
- Name one thing you can see.  
- Blink slowly and notice your breath.  

You are still here. That matters.

---

## 🌼 Step 2:Affirmations

🌬️ Say this to yourself (out loud if you can):

- Fog is **temporary**.  
- I am **not failing**.  
- I do not need to “snap out of it.”  
- My worth is **not** tied to my mental clarity.  
- I’m right here, even in the fog.

---

## 🪞 Step 3: Ask “What’s Missing?”

Fog can be your brain asking for help:

- 😴 Did I sleep okay last night?  
- 💧 Have I had water today?  
- 🍞 Have I eaten something nourishing?  
- 📱 Have I been staring at screens too long?  
- 🧠 Am I overloaded with too many thoughts?

It’s okay if the answer is *yes* to any. You’re not in trouble.

---

## 🧸 Step 4: Let Your Body Lead

Small movements can help clear the fog:

- ✋ Rub your hands together for 10 seconds.  
- 🧍‍♀️ Name 3 things you can *see*, *hear*, and *touch*.  
- ☕ Sip a cold drink and describe the taste out loud.  
- 🧦 Move to a different seat or room.  
- 📦 Stretch, lift something, tap your fingers.
