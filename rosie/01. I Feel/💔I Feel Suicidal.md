# 💔 **I'm Having Thoughts About Suicide / Self Harm**

Rose. 🖤  
If you're here right now...  
You are *already doing something brave.*

Let's check in.  
Take a breath.  
Look at the numbers below and find the one that feels the most true right now.  
Then follow the step next to it.  

You are *not alone.* You are *not broken.* You are *still here.*  
That means there is still hope.

---

## 💡 LEVELS + ACTIONS

### **1 – I’m so happy it almost feels unreal**
> Keep enjoying the moment. Write it down. You may need it later.  
📝 [[📝 Write or Draw How You Feel|Write or draw how you feel]]

---

### **2 – I feel good, and things are looking up**
> Try a gratitude list or tell someone how you're doing.  
💬 [Send a message to Jazz](📞%20Talk%20to%20Someone%20You%20Trust.md)
🧠 [[💬 I want to read affirmations|Read affirmations]]

---

### **3 – I’m okay. Suicide / Self Harm isn’t on my mind**
> Do something you enjoy. Reinforce stability.  
🎧 [[🎧 Listen to Music|Listen to music]]
🧸 [Hold something soft](🧸%20Hold%20a%20Soft%20Object%20or%20Stuffed%20Animal.md)  

---

### **4 – I joke about suicide / self harm but don’t mean it**
> This is stress talking. Let’s vent or release pressure.  
🪵 [[🪵 Grounding Steps|Try grounding steps]]
📝 [[📝 Write or Draw How You Feel|Write a letter to your stress and throw it away]]

---

### **5 – I keep having suicidal / self harm thoughts, but I’m not acting**
> Distraction and comfort are good tools right now.  
📺 [[Watch a comfort show or clip]]
🎧 [[🎧 Listen to Music|Listen to music]]
🛏️ [[🛏️ Wrap Up in a Blanket|Wrap up in a blanket]]

---

### **6 – I’m thinking about suicide / self harm seriously. I can still distract myself**
> You are in danger, love. This is when we reach out.  
📞 [Call or text Jazz NOW]
💬 Say: *“I'm at a 6. I need help before it gets worse.”*

---

### **7 – I can’t stop the thoughts. I feel like I want to give up**
> This is serious. You’re slipping. You need connection.  
🧠 [[💬 I want to read affirmations|Read 3 affirmations aloud]]
📞 [Tell Jazz you're at a 7. Let her help you.]
🧸 [[🧸 Hold a Soft Object or Stuffed Animal|Go to your safe space. Hold something.]]

---

### **8 – I want to make a plan, but I’m stopping myself**
> You need someone with you. Right now.  
🚨 Call Jazz or another trusted person and say:  
> *“I am at an 8. I can’t be alone.”*  
💡 Sit near a safe object, light, or sound that reminds you of being alive.  
📎 [[🧠 The Voices Are Lying|Click here to read “The Voices Are Lying”]]

---

### **9 – I am actively planning. I’m saying goodbye.**
> This is a life-threatening crisis.  
🚨 Call Jazz, 911, or a crisis hotline **now.**  
Tell them:  
> “I am at a 9. I am making a plan to end my life.”  
🚪 **Do not be alone. Stay visible. Let someone see you.**

---

### **10 – I am actively trying to end my life**
> You need emergency help. You are in danger.  
🚨 Call 911 or go to the ER **immediately.**  
If you can’t call, **hand your phone to someone.**  
Don’t wait. Don’t hide.  
You are loved. You deserve to live.  
💬 [Click here if you are afraid of help](voices.md)

---

### 🖤 You are not alone.
This guide cannot replace medical help, but it can *be your anchor until help arrives.*  
You are real.  
You are loved.  
You are worth saving.
