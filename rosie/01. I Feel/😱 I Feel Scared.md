## ✨ Step 1: Check In — "Am I physical safe right now?"

**If the answer is NO or I Don't Know:**  
- Make yourself safe first:  
  - 🚗 If you’re in the car → Lock the doors  
  - 🏠 If you’re at home → Go to your bedroom or plushie corner  
  - 🌆 If we’re in public → Walk in front of Jazz so I can watch over you 
  - 💔 If you are a danger to yourself → Go here **NOW** [[🔪 I Feel Like I Should Hurt Myself]]

Once you feel safer (even a little bit), go to **Step 2** below.
