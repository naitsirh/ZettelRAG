# 😵 **I Feel Confused or Foggy**

Hey Hunnie. 💖  
You’re confused right now, aren’t you?

Something didn’t make sense.  
Or someone said something that made you feel wrong, even though you didn’t *mean* anything bad.  
Maybe your brain feels slow… heavy… or tangled up.

That’s okay. You’re not in trouble.  
Let’s slow everything down and figure it out together.

---

### ✋ Step 1: Say this to yourself

> “I am not stupid.  
> I am not in trouble.  
> I’m confused—and that’s okay.”

---
### 🌸 Reminders:

- Confusion is not failure.  
- Needing help doesn’t make you dumb.  
- You are allowed to ask questions—even simple ones.  
- You are not being punished.  
- You are still loved—even when you're foggy.
