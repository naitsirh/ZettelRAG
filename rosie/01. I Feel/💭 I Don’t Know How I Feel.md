# 💭 I Don’t Know How I Feel

Hi baby. 💖  
It’s okay if you don’t have words right now.  
Not knowing is still a real state to be in.  
Your heart is still speaking — we just need to listen in a softer way.

---

## 🌊 What This Might Feel Like:

- “I feel like I’m on autopilot.”  
- “It’s like I’m underwater, but I can still breathe.”  
- “I’m not sad, but I want to cry.”  
- “I feel disconnected from everything.”  
- “I don’t feel *bad*, but I don’t feel *okay* either.”  
- “The world is happening and I’m just… watching.”  

---

## 💌 You’re Not Broken.

This can be **emotional shutdown** or **dissociation**.  
It happens when your heart is tired, overwhelmed, or protecting itself.

We don’t have to force a label — we’ll *find it together*.

---

## 🧭 Step 1: Anchor to the Now

- Touch something near you.  
- Name one thing you can see.  
- Blink slowly and notice your breath.  

You are still here. That matters.

---

## 💖 Step 2: Reminders Just for You:

🌬️ Say this to yourself (out loud if you can):

>- I am **not broken**.  
>- I am allowed to be uncertain.  
>- Even without a label, My feelings matter.  
>- This moment will not last forever.  
>- I am still **my person**.


---

## 🖐 Step 3: Let’s See Where It Lives

Your body can give clues, even when your mind is blank:

- **Chest feels heavy?** Might be sadness, fear, or guilt.  
- **Jaw clenched or fists tight?** Might be anger or frustration.  
- **Stomach twisting or fluttering?** Might be anxiety, dread, or shame.  
- **Head buzzing or foggy?** Might be confusion or overload.  

---

- Place it on a little shelf in front of you.  
- Look at all the shapes — which one feels biggest?
