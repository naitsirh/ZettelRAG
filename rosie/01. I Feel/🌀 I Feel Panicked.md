# 🌀 **I Feel Panicked**

Okay Rosie. 💖  
You’re having a panic episode, aren’t you?  

That’s okay. I’m right here with you.  
You are *panicking*, and your brain is firing alarms that feel too big.  
Let’s slow everything down together.

---

## 🛑 Step 1: Name It

Say this out loud (or in your head if speaking feels too hard):

> “This is a panic attack.  
> I am not in danger.  
> My body is reacting to fear, not reality.”

You’re safe. Say it again if you need to.

---

## 🌬️ Step 2: Breath Reset

We won’t go deep yet — we’ll go steady.  

- Sit or stand still.  
- Put your hand on your chest or stomach.  
- Breathe in slowly through your nose (count 1…2…3…).  
- Hold for 2 seconds.  
- Breathe out slowly through your mouth (count 1…2…3…4…5…).  

Do this **three times**.

---

## ✋ Step 3: Ground Your Body

Pick **one** of these:

### 🧊 Cold Help
- Hold something cold in your hand  
- Press it to your face or neck  
- Focus only on the cold — it means you are *here.*

### 📦 Object Naming
- Name **5** things you can see  
- Name **4** things you can touch  
- Name **3** things you can hear  
- Name **2** things you can smell  
- Name **1** thing you can taste or imagine

---

## 💌 Step 4: Remember

Say one or more of these to yourself:

> “I am not in danger.”  
> “I am having a panic response, and it will pass.”  
> “I will not die from this.”  
> “I am safe right now.”  
> “I am doing well.”  
> “I am proud of myself.”
