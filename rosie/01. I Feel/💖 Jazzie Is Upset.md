# 💖 **Jazzie Is Upset**

Hi Hunny Bunnie. 💗  
I know you might be worried right now.  
You noticed Jazzie is upset — and your brain might be telling you it’s **about you.**  

Let’s slow down together.  
Sometimes, Jazzie’s feelings have *nothing* to do with you.  
Sometimes, she’s upset about work, life, pain, or something that happened earlier.  
You are not automatically in trouble.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:
- You notice Jazzie is quieter, sighing, or moving differently  
- You hear her voice sound sharper or see her face look serious  
- Your stomach feels tight, and you want to ask “Are you mad at me?”  
- You can’t stop replaying what you last said or did  

---

## 💌 You’re Not Alone:
- Jazzie still loves you, even when she’s upset  
- Feeling worried about her mood means you care  
- Asking once is okay — but asking over and over might make her *more* upset because she has to keep answering  
- She wants you to feel safe without having to ask ten times

---

## 🌼 Affirmations:
- “Jazzie’s mood is not always about me.”  
- “I am still loved, even if she is upset.”  
- “I don’t need to fix everything right now.”  
- “I can give her space and still be close.”

---

## 🪄 What To Do Instead:
1. **Ask once** — “Are you upset with me?”  
2. If she says **no**, believe her.  
3. Give her a little space — you can still stay in the room, just don’t crowd her.  
4. Distract yourself: 
	1. [[🎧 Listen to Music]]
	2. [[🪵 Grounding Steps]]
	3. [[📝 Write or Draw How You Feel]]
	4. [[🧸 Hold a Soft Object or Stuffed Animal]]
5. Wait for her to come to you when she’s ready.

---

📌 **Tip:** If you still feel worried after a while, [[📝 Write or Draw How You Feel|write your thoughts down instead of asking again. ]]
You can share the note later if you still want to talk.
