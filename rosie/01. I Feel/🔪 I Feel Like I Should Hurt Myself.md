# 🔪 **I Feel Like I Should Hurt Myself**

Baby Girl. 💖  
Are you thinking about hurting yourself right now?

Is your brain telling you:
- “I messed up. I deserve this.”  
- “I have to punish myself.”  
- “I ruined everything. I have to fix it by hurting myself.”

Those are *not truths.*  
Those are **lies made from abuser.**  

> This pain will pass.  
> This guilt will soften.  
> You are already forgiven.  
> You are still loved.

Let’s sit together and gently undo them.

---

### 🛑 First: Say this out loud or in your head:

> “I am in pain. But I don’t have to cause more pain.  
> I made a mistake. But that does not mean I deserve to suffer.”

---

### ✋ Step 1: Do a safety check

- ❓ Are you actively planning to hurt yourself?  
  - 🚨 [[💔I Feel Suicidal|Click here right now]]

- ❓ Did you already hurt yourself but want help stopping?  
  - 💬 [Tell someone you trust](talk_someone.md)

- ❓ Are you thinking about pain but don’t want to act?  
  - ❤️ Stay here. You’re in the right place.

---
### 💖 Step 2: Say these truths to yourself:

- I am not a burden.  
- I do not have to be punished to be forgiven.  
- I am not the worst thing I’ve done.  
- Hurting myself will not make this better.  
- My pain is real—but I do not have to bleed it out.  
- Jazz loves me *even when I mess up.*

---
### 🌬️ Step 3: Gentle coping instead:

If you need to feel something **right now**, try:
-  [[🖐️ Hold Something Cold]]
- Snapping a rubber band on your wrist ***once***  
- Drawing on your skin with a pen instead of a blade  
- [[🧱 Hit a Pillow or Cushion]]

These give sensation—**without punishment.**  
Your body deserves care, not injury.

---
### 🌿 Step 4: What do you need right now?

Choose what sounds best to your heart:

- 🧸 [[🧸 Hold a Soft Object or Stuffed Animal|Comfort: Hold a plushie]]
- [[🛏️ Wrap Up in a Blanket]]
- 💬 [[📞 Talk to Someone You Trust|Connection: Talk to someone safe]]
- 🎧[[🎧 Listen to Music|Distraction: Listen to music or a playlist]]
- 🧠[[💬 I want to read affirmations|Truth: Read affirmations]]
