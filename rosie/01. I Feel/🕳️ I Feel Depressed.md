# 🕳️ I Feel Depressed / Empty

Hi love. 💖  
I know this one hits you fast.  
You can have a good day and still end up here — that doesn’t make you weak.  
It just means your brain’s chemicals aren’t lining up right now.  

You are not broken.  
You are not failing.  
You are still you — even if it feels hollow inside.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:

- “Everything feels flat and grey.”  
- “I can’t find a reason for it.”  
- “I’m not sad, I’m just… gone.”  
- “My chest feels hollow.”  
- “I was fine earlier, and now I’m not.”  
- “I don’t understand why this is happening.”

---

## 💌 You’re Not Alone

This isn’t your fault.  
It’s not a choice.  
And it doesn’t erase the good moments you had today.  

Your brain can dip even when nothing “caused” it — that’s just how depression works sometimes.  
I’m here. You don’t have to climb out right now.

---

## 🌼 Affirmations for the Hollow Moments

You don’t have to believe them. Just let them sit near you:

- “I am not a problem.”  
- “This feeling will not last forever.”  
- “I am loved, even if I can’t feel it right now.”  
- “I am not a burden.”  
- “I am worth staying for.”  

If you want a few more:

- “My worth is not measured by my mood.”  
- “I am allowed to take up space, even when I feel empty.”  
- “It’s okay to just exist right now.”  
- “I don’t need to get over it now—I will in time.” 
- “The voices tell lies.”  
- “I am not hard to love.”  


---

## 🫂 Reminders

- Depression lies — it is *not* the truth of who you are.  
- You are still here, and that matters more than anything.  
- You don’t need to force happiness — rest is enough.  

I’m right here until it passes.  
And it will. 🖤
