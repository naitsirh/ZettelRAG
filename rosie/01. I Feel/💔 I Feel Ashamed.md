# 💔 **I Feel Ashamed / Guilty**

Hi love. 💖  
If you’re here, something happened that made you feel small.  
Maybe you said something you didn’t mean.  
Maybe you made a mistake.  
Maybe nothing even happened — and you still feel like you did something wrong.

Shame and guilt can feel heavy.  
But they are not proof that you are bad.  
It’s proof that you care.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:

- “I feel dirty or bad inside.”  
- “I keep replaying what I said or did.”  
- “I feel like everyone is judging me.”  
- “I feel like I need to hide.”  
- “I feel like I ruined everything.”  
- “I feel like I don’t deserve love right now.”  
- “I feel like I should be punished.”

---

## 💌 You’re Not Alone

Shame and Guilt are signals, not a sentence.  
It’s your mind saying, *“I care about how I affect the world.”*  
That’s not evil. That’s not broken.  
That’s human. And it means you still want to be good.


---

## 🖐️ Step 1: Try Touch and Body Awareness

> If your mind is drowning in “what I did,” your body can remind you: “I’m still here.”

- Rub your chest gently in circles and say: “I’m sorry. I love you.”  
- Place your hand over your heart and feel it beating.  
- Name **five things in the room** without judging them.  
- Touch something warm or soft and focus only on how it feels.

---

## 🌼 Step 2: Affirmations

Say these slowly, out loud if you can:  

> “I made a mistake. But I am not a mistake.”  
> “I do not need to be punished to be loved.”  
> “I am still worthy—even when I mess up.”  
> “I am already loved. I don’t have to fix anything first.”

---

## ☀️ Step 3: Choose a Gentle Task

These are small steps. Choose one, or none.  
You’re in control. You don’t need to “earn” peace. You can just take it.

- 🧸 [[🧸 Hold a Soft Object or Stuffed Animal|Hug a pillow or plushie like it’s someone who forgives you.  ]]  
- 📖 Read something that reminds you of who you are at your best.  
- 🎧 [[🎧 Listen to Music|Play a song that makes you feel understood.]]  
- 🫂 Sit near someone safe, even silently.
