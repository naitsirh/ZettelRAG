# 👁️ I See Something Strange

Hi baby. 💖  
You’re seeing something that doesn’t look right, aren’t you?  

Let’s slow down and check together.  
You are not in trouble. You are not bad.  
Your eyes are showing you something — but that doesn’t mean it’s real.  
Whatever you see, you’re still safe with me.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:
- A person, figure, or shadow that no one else notices  
- Shapes or movement in the corner of your eye  
- Faces changing or warping  
- People moving where no one should be  
- Something that feels real… but also wrong  

This is called a **visual hallucination**.  
It’s not your fault. Your brain is sending a strong message, and we’re just listening together.

---

## 🌼 Affirmations
You don’t have to believe these — just read them slowly:  
- “I am safe right now.”  
- “This will pass.”  
- “What I see is not in control of me.”  
- “I can choose how I respond.”  
- “I am loved, no matter what my eyes show me.”
