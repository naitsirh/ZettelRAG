# 😡 **I Feel Angry / Frustrated**

Oh love. ❤️  
It’s okay to feel angry.  
Let’s take a breath together first. 🌬️

---

## 🌬️ Step 1: Breathe Before Anything Else

> Anger makes your body hot and tense. Breathing can help cool it down before it overflows.

- Sit or stand still.  
- Put your hand on your chest or stomach.  
- Breathe in slowly through your nose (count 1…2…3…).  
- Hold it gently for 2 seconds.  
- Breathe out slowly through your mouth (count 1…2…3…4…5…).  

Do this **3 times**.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like

- “I’m so mad I can’t think straight.”  
- “Everything is irritating me.”  
- “I want to yell, hit, or break something.”  
- “I feel heat in my face or chest.”  
- “I can’t calm down no matter what I do.”  
- “I feel tense all over.”  
- “I want to make it stop right now.”

---

## 💌 You’re Not Wrong

Anger is not bad.  
It’s a signal from your body that something feels wrong, unfair, or unsafe.  
You are **allowed** to feel it. We just want to help you hold it without it burning you.

---

## 🌿 Step 3: Name It Gently

You don’t need to agree with all of these — just notice what feels close.

- I feel **disrespected**.  
- I feel **misunderstood**.  
- I feel **trapped**.  
- I feel **ignored**.  
- I feel **like no one is listening**.  
- I feel **like I can’t say what I need**.  

If some of these feel right, keep going — we can work through it together.

---

## 🖐️ Step 4: Release the Energy Safely

> Anger builds up in your body like steam. We’re going to let some of it out without hurting yourself or others.

- Hold something cold — an ice cube, frozen peas, a cold drink can.  
- Hit or punch a pillow or cushion.  
- Stomp your feet hard on the floor.  
- Scream into a pillow or sing loudly to a song.  
- Do 10 quick jumping jacks or push-ups.  

---

## 🌼 Step 5: Affirmations

🌬️ Say this to yourself (out loud if you can):

> "I am feeling anger, but I am still in control."  
> "It’s okay to take a break."  
> "My feelings are valid, even if they’re loud."  
> "I am safe to feel what I feel."

---

## ☀️ Step 6: Choose a Calming Task

These are gentle shifts to help you cool down. Choose **one**, or none. You’re in control.

- 🎧 [[🎧 Listen to Music|Play a song that matches your mood, then one that calms it.]]  
- ✏️ [[📝 Write or Draw How You Feel|Get the words or shapes out of your head.]]  
- [[🖐️ Hold Something Cold]]
- 📺 Watch a show that makes you laugh.  
- 🚿 Take a quick shower — feel the water on your skin.
