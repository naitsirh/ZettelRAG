# 🤲 **I Feel Something Strange on My Body**

Hi Rosie. 💖  
Are you feeling something crawling on your skin?  
A tap, a grab, or a pressure that shouldn't be there?  
It’s okay to say yes. Let’s check together.

---

### ✨ Ask yourself:

- Does it feel like bugs, crawling, or itching beneath your skin?  
- Does it feel like someone is touching you—but no one’s near?  
- Are you feeling poked, brushed, or pulled when no one touched you?

These are called **tactile hallucinations.**  
They are not your fault. They don’t mean anything bad about you.  
They’re just loud messages from your nervous system.


---

### 🛠️ What you can try right now:

- Gently press your fingers into a real object: your shirt, a blanket, the car seat  
	- 🧸 [Hold a soft object or stuffed animal](🧸%20Hold%20a%20Soft%20Object%20or%20Stuffed%20Animal.md)  
- Use a **cold object** like a soda can or damp cloth to interrupt the sensation  
	- 🖐️ [[🖐️ Hold Something Cold|Hold something cold]] 
- Move your arms or legs slowly, like you’re brushing off something  
- Place your hand on your own chest or cheek—*only if it feels safe*  
	- (You don’t have to touch if you’re not ready. Just *breathe.*)
- Whisper out loud:  
> *“This is not real. I am in control of my body.”*

---

🌸 *What you feel is real to you—but it’s not always true.*  
You are not in danger.  
You are not dirty.  
You are not being touched without your consent.  
You are still safe.  
You are still you.
