# 🤢 **I Feel Sick or In Pain**

Darling. 💖  
First, Sit.
If you’re here, that means your body is asking for help.  
Let’s slow down and take care of it, together.

---

### ✨ Step 1: Check Your Body

- Are you in pain right now?  
- Are you feeling dizzy, heavy, or shaky?  
- Do your legs feel restless?  
- Is your head hurting or spinning?

You don’t need to push through it.  
Your body is *not the enemy.* It’s asking you to listen.

---

### 🛑 **Important: You are NOT a burden.**

You are allowed to rest.  
You are *supposed* to rest.  
You are not lazy.  
You are not falling behind.  
You are still worthy—just as you are.

---

### 💊 Step 2: Did you take your medicine?

You’re supposed to take your meds **at 8 AM and 8 PM.**  
Please check.

- If you forgot → It’s okay. Take it now if it’s safe.  
- If you already took it → No need to take more. Just rest now.  
- If you want to flush it or are afraid of it → [[💊 I Don’t Want to Take My Meds]]

Your medicine helps you feel better. It is not poison.  
You are not being tricked.  
You are safe. I promise.

---
### 🕯️ You do not need to “earn” rest.  
Rest is your right.  
You are doing enough.  
You are enough.


---
