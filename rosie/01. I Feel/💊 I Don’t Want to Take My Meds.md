# 💊 I Don’t Want to Take My Meds

Hi, my love.  
If you’re here, it’s okay. Let’s pause and **breathe first**.

You might feel scared, unsure, or even angry.  
You might think:

- “I don’t need them today.”  
- “They make me feel weird.”  
- “They change who I am.”  
- “I’m not sick right now.”  
- “I don’t trust them.”  
- “What if this is a trap?”

Or maybe you don’t know why—you just feel **wrong** inside.

But this part is **true**:

🗣️ **The voices may lie about your meds.**  
Even if you don't believe its the voices, it is. 
You don't want to hurt yourself.
They say things that feel real or like its your own voice. 
But that doesn’t mean they’re true.  
They want you to be *alone* in your mind.  
Your medicine helps protect you from them.

---

## 🌫️ Why Might This Happen?

When you're scared to take your meds, it might be because:

- The voices are **louder** than usual.  
- You feel **confused**, **paranoid**, or **unsafe**.  
- Something triggered a **trauma response**.  
- You feel like meds are a form of **control**.  
- You just don’t feel like **yourself.**

It doesn’t mean you’re failing.  
It doesn’t mean you’re broken.

---

## 🛑 Important Truths:

- 💊 You *still need your meds* even when you feel okay.  
- 🧠 Skipping them **makes the voices stronger**.  
- 🩹 Your meds help your brain **heal and rest**.  
- 🫂 Taking them is **not punishment**—it’s self-care.  
- ⛑️ When you skip meds, it can **endanger you and others.**  
  (That’s not shame. That’s just the truth we live with.)

---

## 🔁 Reframing Thoughts (Say These Out Loud)

- “I’m scared, but I can still choose to take them.”  
- “The voices are loud, but they are not the boss of me.”  
- “This is my brain trying to protect me in a broken way.”  
- “I take my meds because I **deserve peace.**”  
- “Taking my medicine is **me loving myself.**”

---

## 🧸 What You Can Do Right Now:

### 👂 Step 1: Listen to the fear—but don’t obey it.

Say:  
> “I hear you, fear. But I choose safety.”

You can say [[🍯 Syrup]] if it’s all too much.  
You can say [[🌶️ Cinnamon]] if you're worried someone’s mad.  
You can say [[🧁 Cupcakes]] if you're afraid of being in trouble.

---

### 🥣 Step 2: Gentle Return

If you're okay with it…  
Can you take a **tiny sip of water** with your medicine?  
Hold a [[🧸 Hold a Soft Object or Stuffed Animal]] while you do.  
Play a song you like. Ask someone to stay with you.

If you take it, say:  
> “I did it. I took care of me.”  
> “I’m proud of myself. Even if it was scary.”

---

## 🌼 Step 3: Affirmations

🌬️ Say this to yourself (out loud if you can):

I am not weak for needing help.  
I am  not broken for needing medicine.  
I am  **brave** for facing the fear—even when my own brain is loud.  

And no matter what the voices say:  
I am **deeply loved.**  
I am **not alone.**  
I am **safe now.**
