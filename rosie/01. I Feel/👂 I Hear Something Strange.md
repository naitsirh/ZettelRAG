# 👂 I Hear Something Strange

Hi baby. 💖  
You’re hearing something that doesn’t feel quite right, aren’t you?  
That can be unsettling — but you are safe here with me.  
You’re not in trouble, and you’re not doing anything wrong.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:
- Someone calling your name when no one is nearby  
- Whispering, mumbling, or conversations you can’t quite make out  
- Clear voices talking to you (or about you) when no one else is around  
- Music, laughter, or a TV/radio sound with no source  
- Sudden bangs, knocks, or footsteps when you’re alone  
- Background noise like a crowd, cafeteria, or traffic in a quiet space  

These are symptoms — not personal failings.  
You are still here. You are still you. You are loved.

## 🌼 Affirmations

You don’t need to believe these right now.  
Just read them slowly, or whisper them to yourself:

- “This is a symptom. It will pass.”  
- “I am in control of my actions.”  
- “I am safe in this moment.”  
- “Hearing things does not make me bad.”  
- “I can choose what to listen to.”  
- “I am loved, even when I’m scared.”
