# 😢 **I Feel Sad**

Hi love. 💖  
It’s okay to feel sad.  
You are safe, and this feeling will pass — even if it doesn’t feel that way right now.

---

## 💧 What This Might Feel Like

- “I feel heavy and slow.”  
- “I can’t stop crying — or I wish I could cry.”  
- “I feel like nothing matters anymore.”  
- “Everything feels hopeless.”  
- “I feel empty, like something’s missing.”  
- “I just want to hide from everything.”

---

## 💌 You’re Not Weak

Sadness is a normal human signal — it tells us we’ve lost something, or something important matters to us.  
It means your heart is working, even if it hurts. 💖

---

## 🌿 Name It Gently

You don’t have to agree with all of these — just notice if something feels close.

- I feel **lonely**.  
- I feel **tired**.  
- I feel **like I lost something important**.  
- I feel **like no one understands**.  
- I feel **like I’m carrying too much**.  

If some of these feel right, keep going — there are ways to hold it safely.

---

## 🌼 Affirmations

🌬️ Say this to yourself (out loud if you can):

> "It’s okay to feel sad — my feelings are valid."  
> "I am allowed to rest and heal."  
> "This feeling will pass, even if it’s slow."  
> "I am still loved, even when I don’t feel lovable."

---

## ☀️ Choose a Gentle Task

These are soft shifts to help your heart feel less heavy.  
Choose **one**, or none. You’re in control.

- 🎧 [[🎧 Listen to Music|Put on a song that feels like a hug.]]  
- 🛏️ [[🛏️ Wrap Up in a Blanket|Get cozy and feel safe.]]  
- 📝 [[📝 Write or Draw How You Feel|Let it out onto paper — words, colors, or shapes.]]  
- [[🧸 Hold a Soft Object or Stuffed Animal]]
- 🐾 **Pet an Animal** (real or stuffed)
- 📖 **Read Something Comforting** (a short story, quotes, or a cozy book)
- 🍵 **Have a Warm Drink** (tea, cocoa)
- 📷 **Look at Happy Memories** (photos, videos)
- 🌱 **Step Outside for 2 Minutes** (light, fresh air)
