# 🍬 Candy Code Dictionary  
> **A shared language of love, comfort, and clarity**

---

## 📘 What is this?

This is **our secret language**—words that help us navigate emotions, miscommunications, and scary moments without shame or confusion.

Every word here is soft. Every word here is safe.

They’re sweet little signals to **help us connect faster**, **soothe misunderstandings**, and **wrap each other in comfort**—even when things are hard.

---

## 🍭 Word Index

### 🧁 **Cupcake**  
> “You didn’t need to apologize.”  
🧠 Use when someone says sorry for something harmless or unnecessary.  
🔗 [[🧁 Cupcakes]]

---

### 🍬 **Gumdrop**  
> “That made me feel special!”  
🧠 Use when someone says or does something that fills you with joy.  
🔗 [[🍬 Gumdrop]]

---

### 🍫 **Chocolate Cake** *(aka Lucario)*  
> “That’s a trauma trigger—let’s replace the memory.”  
🧠 Used when something triggers PTSD. Replace the power with a positive anchor.  
🔗 [[🍰 Chocolate Cake]]

---

### 🍯 **Syrup**  
> “That’s a little much—can we slow down?”  
🧠 Use to gently set boundaries or when things feel overwhelming or intense.  
🔗 [[🍯 Syrup]]

---

### 🧂 **Salted**  
> “That joke stung a little.”  
🧠 Use to let someone know something they said hurt, even if it was meant to be playful.  
🔗 [[🧂 Salted]]

---

### 🍬 **Jelly Beans**  
> “Are you joking?”  
🧠 Use when you're not sure if someone is being sarcastic or serious.  
🔗 [[🍬 Jelly Beans]]

---

### 🍬 **Cinnamon**  
> “Are you mad at me?”  
🧠 Use when you sense tension and need reassurance.  
🔗 [[🌶️ Cinnamon]]

---

### 🍬 **Marshmallows**  
> “You don’t need to explain yourself.”  
🧠 Use when someone is spiraling trying to justify their actions or choices unnecessarily.  
🔗 [[🍥 Marshmallows]]

---

### 🍬 **Sugar**  
> “I’m not mad at you.” or “That’s okay.”  
🧠 A gentle affirmation word—reassures that things are okay emotionally.  
🔗 [[🍬 Sugar]]
