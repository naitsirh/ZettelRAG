# 🍥 **Marshmallows**

Hi love. 💖  
Are you explaining something… again?  
Are you starting to say:

> “I only did it because…”  
> “It made sense at the time…”  
> “I didn’t mean to—what happened was…”

That’s when someone might say:  
> **“Marshmallows.”**

---

### 🍡 What does “Marshmallows” mean?

**“Marshmallows” means:  
You don’t have to explain.  
You’re not in trouble.  
I trust you. It’s okay.”**

No one’s mad.  
No one’s judging.  
You are allowed to make decisions without a speech.  
You are allowed to exist *without defending yourself.*

---

### 💬 When does someone say “Marshmallows”?

- You’re talking fast to explain something harmless  
- You’re afraid someone will be upset about what you did  
- You’re trying to *justify* your actions so no one thinks you were wrong  
- You’re spiraling and don’t realize you’re trying to protect yourself with words

---

### 🧸 What do I do when I hear “Marshmallows”?

- Pause.  
- Take a deep breath.  
- Say to yourself:  
> “I’m not in danger. I don’t need to explain. I’m still loved.”

- You can smile and say:  
> “Okay.”  
And let it go.

---

### 🎭 Example:

**You say:**  
> “I only said it that way because I was tired and I had a lot going on and I didn’t mean to snap—”

**They say:**  
> “Marshmallows.”  
**You say:**  
> “Oh. Okay.”  
**Then you probably say:**  
> “Sorry…”  
**Then they say:**  
> “[[🧁 Cupcakes]].”  
(And everything is fine.)

---

### 🌸 You are not on trial.

You are not in trouble.  
You don’t have to explain yourself to be safe.  
You are already enough.  
You are already loved.
