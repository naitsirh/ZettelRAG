# 🍰 **Chocolate Cake**

Hi Rosie. 💙  
You saw something blue, didn’t you?

A car maybe.  
A certain shade.  
A reminder that felt like a punch in the stomach.

But look where you are, sweetheart.  
You're here. Not there.  
**He’s gone.**

---

## 🍰 What does “Chocolate Cake” mean?

**“Chocolate Cake” means:  
You saw something that used to hurt you.  
But now we’re taking it back.**

It’s a signal to *reclaim the moment.*

When someone says “Chocolate Cake,” they’re telling you:

> “That color doesn’t belong to him anymore.  
We’ve changed the story.”

---

## 💙 How It Works

Sometimes, your body remembers before your brain can explain.  
You see the blue car and your heart starts racing.  
Your fists clench. Your voice disappears.

So we **say or hear:**

> “Mmm… chocolate cake sounds good.”  
> “Matt would say Lucario is the best blue.”  
> “I wonder if Lucario could drive a car…”

And that’s your cue to come back.  
To remember:  
> You’re not in that car.  
> You’re not in that place.  
> You’re not with him.  
> You’re with us now.

---

## 🎮 Lucario Mode (Optional Upgrade)

When you want extra help, look at your Lucario plush.  
Say out loud:

> “Matt loves blue. Not him.”  
> “Lucario is stronger than memories.”  
> “I am not there. I’m here.”

Let Lucario guard you.  
Let Chocolate Cake sweeten the sharp things.  
Let your new story win.

---

## 💬 When do we use it?

- You flinched when you saw a blue car  
- You looked frozen or your breathing changed  
- You started to cry but didn’t know why  
- You whispered “he had a car like that…”

We say “Chocolate Cake” and the world softens.

---

## 🌸 What do I do when I hear “Chocolate Cake”?

- **Look at something safe** (Lucario, your hand, the floor, Matt)  
- **Breathe in and out 3 times**  
- **Say (out loud or in your mind):**  
> “He doesn’t get to own blue.”  
> “I’m safe. I’m free. I’m here.”

---

## 🍰 Examples

**You say:**  
> “That’s his car… that’s the kind…”  
**They say:**  
> “Chocolate cake sounds great right now.”  
**That means:**  
> “You’re okay. Let’s take back the color.”

---

**You’re not where the fear lives.  
You’re where the healing lives.**  
And it smells like chocolate cake. 🍰
