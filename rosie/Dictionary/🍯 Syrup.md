# 🍯 **Syrup**  
> “That’s a little much—can we slow down?”

---

## 🍯 What does “Syrup” mean?

**“Syrup” is a signal that something is too fast, too much, or too intense.**  
It’s how you say:  
> “I’m not mad. I’m not upset. I just need a second to breathe.”

You might use it when:

- Someone is talking too fast
- The topic is overwhelming
- Too many things are happening at once
- A joke or story feels a little too *big* right now

It’s not a panic word—it’s a **comfort word**.  
Sticky, warm, and slow.

---

## 🧠 Why “Syrup”?

Because syrup moves slowly.  
Because it reminds us of soft mornings, pancakes, warm safety.  
Because slowing down *doesn’t mean stopping.*  
It means making space to feel good again.

---

## 💬 When do we say it?

You might say **“Syrup”** if you’re thinking:

- “Wait—what? I’m lost.”
- “This is a lot of energy all at once.”
- “My brain is racing but I don’t know what to say.”
- “I’m not ready for this part of the conversation yet.”

---

## 🧸 What happens when someone says “Syrup”?

If **you say Syrup**, your loved one should:

1. **Pause immediately**—slow their voice, energy, or tone  
2. **Check in gently** – “Too fast?” “You okay?” “Want me to explain slower?”  
3. **Give you room** – Let you take your time or shift the subject  

If **someone says Syrup to you**, it doesn’t mean you did something wrong.  
It just means the moment got *sticky*, and we need to pour a little slower.

---

## 🍽️ Examples

**You say:**  
> “Wait, Syrup.”  
**They say:**  
> “Got it—too fast?”  
**You say:**  
> “Yeah, can we just talk slower?”  

---

**You say:**  
> “Syrup.”  
**They say:**  
> “Want a break?”  
**You say:**  
> “Yeah. Let me breathe.”

---

## 💡 Tips

- Syrup can also be said *after* something intense—like, “Whew. Syrup.”
- It’s great during loud rooms, fast talking, or group settings.
- If you're not sure why you’re overwhelmed, Syrup is a safe catch-all.

---

## 🫶 Response Phrases

- “Of course—want to sit with me and slow it down?”
- “We can pause. No rush.”
- “Thanks for saying Syrup. You’re doing great.”
