# 🍬 **Gumdrop**  
> “That made me feel special!”

---

## 🍬 What does “Gumdrop” mean?

**Gumdrop** is our way of saying:

> “That moment just made me feel seen, loved, or cared for in a little magical way.”

It’s a happy-squeal-in-a-word.  
It’s a sprinkle of “thank you” with a hug inside.  
It’s the opposite of Salted—pure sugar rush of *positivity*.

---

## 💖 Why “Gumdrop”?

Because gumdrops are small, sweet, and colorful—just like the little things that make our heart jump with joy.  
It’s a way to **notice** when love is *working*, even in tiny doses.

---

## 🍬 When do we say it?

You might say **Gumdrop** when:

- Someone says something really kind or affirming
- A compliment makes you smile in your bones
- They remembered a little detail about you
- You just feel really… loved 🥺

---

## 🫂 What happens when someone says “Gumdrop”?

If **you say Gumdrop**, your loved one should:

1. **Smile big** because yay, they made you happy
2. **Repeat or lean into** what made you feel good
3. Maybe even say **“Gumdrop back!”**

If **someone says Gumdrop to you**, try:

- “Awwww~ I’m so glad!”
- “You *are* special!”
- “Okay, I’m putting that in my heart vault forever.”

---

## 🍽️ Examples

**You say:**  
> “Gumdrop.”  
**They say:**  
> “Hehe~ what did I do right this time?”

---

**You say:**  
> “That was a total Gumdrop moment.”  
**They say:**  
> “Aww! You’re my favorite flavor~”

---

**They say:**  
> “Gumdrop?”  
**You say:**  
> “Really?? Yayyy~” *(happy dance encouraged)*

---

## 💡 Tips

- Say it *as soon* as you feel it—even if it’s just “Gumdrop!” and a smile.
- It helps reinforce good moments and makes everyone feel better.
- Use it to celebrate tiny victories too, like “I made it through the day!” → “Gumdrop!!”

---

## 💬 Response Phrases

- “You deserve Gumdrops every day.”
- “You’re my little Gumdrop.”
- “Okay, that made my heart sparkle.”
