# 🍬 **Jelly Beans**

Hi love. 💖  
Did someone just say something that confused you?  
It sounded serious, but something felt… weird?  
That’s when you can ask:

> **“Jelly Beans?”**

---

### 🍭 What does “Jelly Beans” mean?

**“Jelly Beans” means:  
“Was that a joke? Are you being sarcastic or serious?”**

It’s a soft way to check.  
It’s safe to say.  
It helps your brain know what’s real and what’s just playful.

---

### 💬 How to use “Jelly Beans”:

If someone says something that makes you panic or feel unsure, you can say:

> “Jelly Beans?”  
or  
> “Is that Jelly Beans?”

If they answer:
- “Yes, I’m joking”  
- “Jelly Beans!”  
- or they laugh gently and explain—

✨ *Then you know it was a joke. You’re okay. No one is mad. Nothing bad is happening.*

---

### 🚨 But what if they say “No” or act serious?

Then it’s time to check in:

- 🧠 Ask: “Did I hurt someone?”  
- 💬 [[📞 Talk to Someone You Trust|Ask Jazz or someone you trust what to do]]
- 🪵 [[🪵 Grounding Steps|Try grounding steps if your brain starts to spiral]]

---

### 🎭 Examples:

**They say:**  
> “Wow, great job. You broke it again.”

You feel upset. So you say:  
> “Jelly Beans?”

**They say:**  
> “Jelly Beans, baby. I’m just teasing.”  
You can relax. They weren’t mad.

---

**They say:**  
> “I guess I’ll just go, then.”

You feel abandoned. So you say:  
> “Jelly Beans?”  

**They say:**  
> “Oh no! That was a joke—I’m not going anywhere.”

---

### 🌸 Jelly Beans helps you *check without fear.*

You don’t have to guess what people mean.  
You’re not wrong for taking things seriously.  
But now you have a way to *ask*—and that’s beautiful.
