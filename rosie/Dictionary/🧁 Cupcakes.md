# 🧁 **Cupcakes**

Hi love. 💖  
You might have just said “sorry”—but… you didn’t need to.  
That’s why someone said:  
> **“Cupcake.”**

---

### 🍰 What does “Cupcakes” mean?

**“Cupcakes” means:  
You didn’t do anything wrong. There’s no reason to apologize.**

You’re okay.  
You didn’t hurt anyone.  
It wasn’t a problem.  
It was *sweet* of you to care, but no one is upset.

---

### ✨ When do people say “Cupcakes”?

- When you say “sorry” but no one was mad or upset  
- When you apologize for something small or harmless  
- When you’re just trying to be polite, but think you did something wrong

---

### 💬 Examples

**You say:**  
> “Sorry, I dropped my spoon.”  
**We say:**  
> “Cupcakes. It’s not a big deal.”

**You say:**  
> “Sorry I touched your arm.”  
**We say:**  
> “Cupcakes! You’re totally okay.”

**You say:**  
> “Sorry for talking too much.”  
**We say:**  
> “Cupcakes. We *want* to hear you talk.”

---

### 💡 What should I do when I hear “Cupcakes”?

- Pause.  
- Take a breath.  
- Say to yourself:  
> “Oh… I didn’t do anything wrong. I’m still loved.”

---

### 🌸 You don’t have to apologize for existing.  
You are not a bother.  
You are not a burden.  
You are a sweet little cupcake—and we love you just as you are.
