# 📋 Too Many Tasks / Things To Do

Hi love, it looks like your brain is going fast right now—  
Like there's **too much to do**, and **not enough time**, and **you don’t even know where to start.**  
That’s okay. We’re going to take care of this **one step at a time**.

---

## 💬 Step 1: Say it Out Loud

Say or think:  
🗣️ _“I don’t have to do everything right now.”_  
🗣️ _“I can do one thing at a time.”_  
🗣️ _“It’s okay if some things wait.”_

---

## 🧠 Step 2: Clear the Brain Fog

Do any of these to calm the brain chaos:

- ✍️ Write down every single thing in your head, like a brain dump.  
- 🧹 Look around: Can you do a 1-minute tidy of your space?  
- 🔕 Turn off extra noise—close tabs, silence phone, ask for quiet.

---

## 🧩 Step 3: Use the Puzzle Method

Let’s sort all those tasks like puzzle pieces.

1. What’s **urgent**?  
2. What’s **important** but can wait?  
3. What’s **not important** and can be skipped?

Just pick **one** thing from the urgent list.  
Do it slow. Do it small.  
**That is enough.**

---

## 🧸 Step 4: Ask for Help

You don’t have to do it alone. You can say:

> _“Can you help me figure out where to start?”_  
> _“Can I do this with you beside me?”_  
> _“Can we talk while I do this so I don’t feel alone?”_

Let people love you. You don’t have to earn it. 💕

---

## 💗 Step 5: Reminders

- You are not lazy.  
- You are not a failure.  
- Your brain is tired, not broken.  
- You are **allowed to rest.**
