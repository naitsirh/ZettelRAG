# 🧩 **I Don’t Understand What I Did Wrong**

Hi RoRo. 💖  
Something happened, and now someone’s upset or confused.  
And you’re thinking:

> “But I didn’t mean to do anything wrong…”  
> “I’m not mad—why are they saying I sound like I am?”  
> “I feel like I messed up, but I don’t know how.”

It’s okay.  
You are not in trouble.  
You’re not bad.  
You’re just confused. And that’s okay.

---

### ✋ Step 1: Say this out loud or in your head:

> “I don’t have to understand everything right now.  
> I am still worthy, even if I made a mistake.”

---

### 💬 Step 2: Let’s look at the pieces

Ask yourself gently:

- Did someone say I had a "tone" or looked upset—but I wasn’t? 
- Did I use words that made someone feel hurt, even though I didn’t mean to?  
- Did I forget to do something important, but didn’t realize it?  
- Did I try to explain myself, but it made things worse?

If yes—it's okay.  
Intent and impact are different.  
You are still a good person.

---

### ✨ Step 3: Things to remember:

- You can hurt someone **without meaning to.** That doesn't make you evil.  
- You can have a tone that sounds upset—even if you feel fine inside.  
- You can *sound sharp or loud* because of confusion, stress, or tone of voice.  
- You are **not stupid** for not catching it. You're learning.
- - 💬 [[📞 Talk to Someone You Trust|Ask Jazz or someone safe to explain calmly. They will happily]]

---
### 🌸 You are not bad.

You didn’t mean to hurt anyone.  
You are allowed to learn without being punished.  
You are allowed to ask questions.  
You are not broken—you’re just human.
