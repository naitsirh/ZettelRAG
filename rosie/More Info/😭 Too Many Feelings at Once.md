# 😭 **Too Many Feelings at Once**

Baby. 💖  
It’s okay if you’re feeling *everything* all at the same time.  
Sometimes the heart gets full—*too full*—and we can’t tell what’s loudest.

We don’t have to fix them all.  
We just need to figure out which one needs attention first.

---

## 🫧 What This Might Feel Like:

- “I feel like I’m drowning in feelings.”  
- “Everything hits me at once and I can’t untangle it.”  
- “My brain keeps bouncing between emotions.”  
- “I feel like crying, yelling, hiding, and running away all at the same time.”  

---

## 🖐️ Step 1: Pause and Notice Your Body

> Your body is a map — it will tell you which feeling is shouting the loudest.

- **Chest feels heavy or tight?** Might be sadness, fear, or guilt.  
- **Jaw clenched / fists tight?** Might be anger or frustration.  
- **Stomach twisting or fluttering?** Might be anxiety, dread, or shame.  
- **Head buzzing or foggy?** Might be confusion or overload.

---

## 🧠 Step 2: Give Each Feeling a “Shape” or “Color”

You don’t have to name them yet — just notice.  
- Pick one spot in your body and ask:  
  > “If this feeling had a color or shape, what would it be?”  
- Imagine placing that shape on a little shelf in front of you.  
- Move to the next feeling and do the same.  

When you’ve shelved them all, pick the one that looks/feels the *biggest*.
