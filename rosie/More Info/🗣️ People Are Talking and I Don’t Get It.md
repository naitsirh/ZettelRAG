# 🗣️ **People Are Talking and I Don’t Get It**

Darling. 💖  
Are people saying things that don’t make sense right now?  
Do you feel left out, confused, or like you said the wrong thing—*even though you didn’t mean to*?

That’s okay.  
You’re not broken.  
You’re not dumb.  
Sometimes our brains get scrambled by too many words, too many feelings, or just *vibes* that don’t make sense.

---

### ✋ Step 1: Say this to yourself

> “I am allowed to be confused.  
> I do not have to pretend I understand.  
> I can ask for help.”

---

### 🧩 Gentle truths to remember:

- You are allowed to not get the joke.  
- If their tone sounds sharp, that doesn’t mean they angry.  
- You can hurt someone’s feelings *without being a bad person.*  
- Asking “What do you mean?” is not rude. It’s brave.
