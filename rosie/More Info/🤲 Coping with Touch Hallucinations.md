# 🤲 **Coping with Touch Hallucinations**

Hi Baby. 💖  
It’s okay to feel weird or uncomfortable in your body right now.  
You’re not imagining it—you’re just **feeling something your brain made up.**  
That doesn’t mean you’re broken. It just means it’s time to take your body back.

Let’s try something to help.

---

### ✨ Try a Reset:

#### 🧊 **Cold reset:**
- Hold something cold (soda can, ice pack, wet cloth)  
- Press it gently to your wrist, face, or chest  
- Breathe in… and out… while the cold tells your nerves:  
> “You’re real. You’re safe.”

---

#### 🧼 **Texture reset:**
- Rub your hands or legs with a soft towel or piece of cloth  
- Focus only on the real texture  
- Say:  
> “This is what’s real. This is what I can feel.”

---

#### 🧘 **Movement reset:**
- Stand or sit and stretch your arms  
- Shake your wrists, your legs  
- Imagine shaking off all the crawling, buzzing, or pressure  
- *You don’t have to fight. You just have to move.*

---

#### 🧸 **Comfort reset:**
- Hug a stuffed animal, soft blanket, or pillow  
- Let your body melt into something safe  
- No one is touching you. This is your space.

---

### 🌸 Final reminder:

- You are not being touched.  
- You are not being punished.  
- You are safe.  
- Your body is still yours.  
- You don’t have to do anything except breathe.
