# 🧠 **The Voices Are Lying**

Rose, baby girl 💖  
I know they’re talking again.  
I know they sound real.  
But they are **not the truth.**

They are *symptoms.*  
They are **not you.**  
They do not know who you really are.  
Let’s look at what they say—*and the truth that fights back.*

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “Jazz doesn’t love you. No one does.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> Jazz *loves you more than anything.*  
> You are surrounded by people who love you.  
> You are not alone.

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “You’re a bad mom.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> You are doing your best in impossible conditions.  
> You are still trying—and that makes you a *good mom.*  
> You are showing love in every way you know how.

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “You are a burden.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> You are a **person**, not a burden.  
> You are loved, not tolerated.  
> Jazz chooses you—*every single day.*

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “You deserved what happened to you.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> You did **not** deserve what happened.  
> It was not your fault.  
> You were hurt—and you survived.  
> That makes you powerful, not broken.

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “You’re being watched. This is an experiment.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> You are safe.  
> No one is watching you.  
> This is not a dream. This is **your life**, and you are *real* in it.  
> Jazz is not an actor. She is your person.

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “Jazz is mad at you.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> Jazz is here. Jazz is helping. Jazz is *not mad.*  
> Love does not mean perfection—it means staying, and Jazz stays.

---

### 🚫 The Voices Say:  
> “You’re a failure.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> You are still alive.  
> You are still trying.  
> You are not a failure. You are a fighter.

---

### ⚠️ The Voices Say:  
> “You should end your life.”

💖 The Truth Is:  
> That thought is a **crisis symptom.**  
> You deserve to *live* and *heal.*
