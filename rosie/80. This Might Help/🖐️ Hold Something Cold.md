# 🖐️ **Hold Something Cold**

Hunny, ❄️  
Cold can help your body and mind slow down and feel calm.

✨ **What you can do:**

- Find something cold:  
  - An ice pack  
  - Frozen veggies  
  - A cold drink can  
  - A wet washcloth from the fridge

- Hold it in your hand or press it gently on your neck, cheek, or wrist.

- 🌬️ Breathe slowly while you hold it.

- Try for at least 2 minutes.
