# 🎧 **Listen to Music**

Babe~ 🎶  
Music can help your mind and body feel calm.  
Let’s try this together.

---

## ✨ **Step 1 — Pick a Song**
- 🎵 Choose something you like, or a playlist you already know.  
- 🎶 Listen for at least **2 songs**.  
- 🛋️ Sit or lie down while you listen.  
- 👂 Focus on:
  - 🥁 The beat  
  - 📝 The words  
  - 🎻 The instruments  

You can sing, hum, or just listen quietly. ❤️
