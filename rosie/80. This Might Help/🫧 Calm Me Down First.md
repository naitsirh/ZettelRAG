## 🖐️ Step 2: Use Your Senses

Let’s ground your body to the world around you. 

- 👁️ **5 things you can see**: Look around and say 3 things you see.  
- 🖐️ **4 things you can touch**: Rub your hands together. Feel your shirt, blanket, or chair. 
- 👂 **3 things you can hear**: Tap your fingers. Hum a song. Listen for the quietest sound.  
- 👃 Smell: Sniff something nearby—lotion, shampoo, clothes.  

- 👅 Taste: Drink water, suck on a candy, or bite something cold.

It’s okay to come back and do these again if you need to.
