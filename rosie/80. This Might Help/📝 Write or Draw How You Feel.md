# 📝 **Write or Draw How You Feel**

Baby girl! 💖  
Let’s try something fun and silly—no pressure!

✨ **Here’s what you can do:**

- Grab a piece of paper or your favorite notebook.
- You can:
  - ✏️ Scribble random shapes or doodles.  
  - 🖍️ Draw what your feelings *might look like* (even just stick figures!)  
  - 🖋️ Write one word or a sentence about how you feel.  
  - 🎨 Draw something totally random (a cat, a flower, a dinosaur!)

🌟 *This doesn’t have to “look good” or “make sense.”*  
You’re just moving your hands and having fun.
