# 🧪 The Observers

**AKA:** “Banana Coats,” “Discount Men in Black,” “Doctor Nope”  
**Last Seen:** Across the street, lurking in crowds, front row in the “Staring Contest You Didn’t Sign Up For.”  

---

## 📜 Profile:
The Observers are *exactly* what they sound like — people in yellow or lab coats who apparently skipped every “don’t be creepy in public” seminar.  

They don’t touch. They don’t talk. They just *watch*. And then — as if they’ve got a remote control for your brain — the voices in your head start up like,  
> “See? You ARE being watched.”  

Congratulations, they just got free rent in your head.  

---

## 🕵️ Known Behaviors:
- Appear in bright yellow or “doctor” getup (because subtlety is dead)  
- Stay just far enough away to make you doubt yourself  
- Never blink. Probably part lizard.  
- Camp out in your line of sight until you’re convinced reality is a scam  

---

## 🚫 Do NOT:
- Keep staring at them (they thrive on it)  
- Let the voices use them as “proof” you’re unsafe  
- Spiral into “what if” land — you’ll just fuel the paranoia  

---

## ✅ Do Instead:
- Break the line of sight — turn your body, move behind something, or leave the area  
- Turn on something grounding — music, a podcast, or a show that demands your attention -   [[🎧 Listen to Music]]
- Remind yourself: “They have no power unless I give it to them.”  
- If you’re at home, physically block windows or mirrors that feed the fear  

---

## 🌼 Affirmations:
- “Their stare means nothing.”  
- “I control my thoughts, not them.”  
- “Even if they’re there, they can’t hurt me.”  
- “My safety is real. They are background noise.”
