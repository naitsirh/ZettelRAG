# 🫣 Faceless People

**AKA:** “Identity Thieves,” “Diet Doppelgängers,” “The No-Eyes Club”  
**Last Seen:** In your house, in your bed, or calling you from the other room like they pay rent here.  

---

## 📜 Profile:
Faceless People are the Walmart knock-off versions of people you actually know.  
They look *almost* like your partner, your kid, or your bestie — except for the tiny detail that their faces are **missing**.  

And because they’re jerks, they don’t just stand there.  
No. They call to you. From the living room. The hallway. The office.  
You *know* better — but your feet start moving anyway.  

---

## 🕵️ Known Behaviors:
- Appear as someone you love… minus the face  
- Call your name or make familiar sounds to lure you closer  
- Hang out in places they *know* you’ll come looking  
- Give you that skin-crawly, wrong-but-I-can’t-stop feeling  

---

## 🚫 Do NOT:
- Go to the sound!!!!!  
- Try to “check” if it’s really them — that’s how they win  
- Engage in conversation. They don’t deserve your time.  

---

## ✅ Do Instead:
- Call or text the real person you think you saw/heard — confirm they’re somewhere else -  [[📞 Talk to Someone You Trust]]
- Stay put and distract yourself with music, a video, or literally *anything* grounding  - [[🎧 Listen to Music]]
- Close doors or block your line of sight to the area they’re in  
- If you *must* pass through, do it without looking at them  

---

## 🌼 Affirmations:
- “If I can’t see their face, they’re not real.”  
- “I don’t chase sounds.”  
- “The real people I love don’t sneak up on me.”  
- “I am in control of where I go.”
