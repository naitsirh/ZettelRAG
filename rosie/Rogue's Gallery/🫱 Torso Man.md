# 🫱 Torso Man

**AKA:** “Mr. No-Legs,” “Ceiling Creeper,” “Wind-Up Chomp Chomp”  
**Last Seen:** Underfoot (literally) or upside down (rude).  

---

## 📜 Profile:
Here we go. The human torso that refuses to mind his business. No legs. No shame. Just *vibes*… and that ridiculous “I-yayayayayay” sound every time his mouth opens and closes.  

Imagine one of those plastic wind-up teeth toys your grandma got you as a “funny” gift — except it scuttles across the floor like it’s late for work and thinks your ankles are on the menu.  

---

## 🕵️ Known Behaviors:
- Scuttles on the ground at full gremlin speed  
- Bites at ankles like they personally wronged him  
- Occasionally forgets gravity and hangs on the ceiling (show-off)  
- Won’t stop with the *I-yayayayayay* noise  

---

## 🚫 Do NOT:
- Leave your feet dangling like bait  
- Try to kick him — he’ll just think it’s playtime  
- Engage in serious conversation (he’s not bright)  

---

## ✅ Do Instead:
- Put your feet up on the bed, couch, or chair  - [[🛏️ Wrap Up in a Blanket]]
- Laugh at the *I-yayayayayay* until it loses all creep factor  
- If he’s on the ceiling, inform him:  
  > “I don’t have time for people with no legs.”  
- Bonus move: Pretend to toss him a chew toy  

---

## 🌼 Affirmations:
- “Torso Man is a wind-up toy, not a threat.”  
- “I can stay above the chaos.”  
- “My ankles are safe.”  
- “No-legs = no power over me.”
