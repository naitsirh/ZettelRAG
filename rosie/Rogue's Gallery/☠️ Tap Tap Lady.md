# ☠️ Tap Tap Lady

**AKA:** “Eggs at Midnight,” “Grandma Wrong-Hour,” “That Bitch Again”  
**Last Seen:** Way too late at night, always when you were cozy.  

---

## 📜 Profile:
Oh look. It’s her. Again.  
The *classic* old lady routine — shows up outside your window after dark, politely(?) tapping like she’s here to borrow sugar and eggs… at 1:47 AM.  

Because obviously that’s when civilized people go grocery shopping, right?  

She’s not here to hurt you — just to ruin your vibe. The tapping is *weirdly polite* but *extremely persistent*. If you try to look for her? Congrats, you just made her night.  

---

## 🕵️ Known Behaviors:
- Taps on the glass at the **wrong time of night**  
- Refuses to use the door like a normal person  
- Somehow always knows when you just got comfortable  
- Stares like she’s waiting for you to break first  

---

## 🚫 Do NOT:
- Go to the window  
- Try to “catch her in the act”  
- Stand there listening like it’s a horror podcast  

---

## ✅ Do Instead:
- Crank up the TV or music until she’s background noise  - [[🎧 Listen to Music]]
- Leave the room and hang out somewhere with no windows  
- Remind yourself this is a repeat offender — she’s boring at this point  
- Bonus move: Eat *her* imaginary eggs and make a cake out of spite  

---

## 🌼 Affirmations:
- “Tap Tap Lady does not get my attention tonight.”  
- “I control the volume in my space.”  
- “Old ladies don’t scare me, even ghosty ones.”  
- “I choose rest over curiosity.”
