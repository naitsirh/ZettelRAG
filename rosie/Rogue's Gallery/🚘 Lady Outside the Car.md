# 🚘 Lady Outside the Car

**AKA:** “Window Wraith,” “Parking Lot Patty,” “Bang-Bang Betty”  
**Last Seen:** Parking lots, dimly lit streets, anywhere your car feels *too quiet*.  

---

## 📜 Profile:
The queen of bad timing. She starts off staring at you *way* across the street — like what the fuck lady — but the second Jazz get out of the car? BAM. She’s suddenly at your window, banging, screaming, doing her best audition for “Jump Scare of the Year.”  

If it’s nighttime, congratulations: she’s going for an Oscar.  

---

## 🕵️ Known Behaviors:
- Starts far away, instantly teleports to your car window  
- Bangs like the world’s angriest door-to-door salesman  
- Screams without warning, 0–100 drama  
- Only works night shift for maximum creep factor  
- Disappears faster than a free sample tray at Costco  

---

## 🚫 Do NOT:
- Rush outside to check (she *wants* the drama)  
- Stare at her too long — she’s like a raccoon, attention makes her bolder  
- Try to argue logic with her (she skipped that class)  

---

## ✅ Do Instead:
- Stay inside your car, building, or store until you’re calm  - [[😱 I Feel Scared]]
- Call someone you trust to be “on the line” until you’re safe  - [[📞 Talk to Someone You Trust]]
- If you’re in the car, lock the doors and put on music or a podcast loud enough to drown her out  - [[😱 I Feel Scared]]
- Practice your best *unimpressed glare* for the security cameras  

---

## 🌼 Affirmations:
- “Lady Outside the Car can’t actually reach me.”  
- “Her noise is just noise.”  
- “I am safe where I am.”  
- “I control the car. I control the space.”
